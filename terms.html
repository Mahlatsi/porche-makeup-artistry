📜 Studio Policy

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Upon booking an appointment with PORCHÈ MAKEUP ARTISTRY, you agree to the following terms and studio policies designed to protect your luxury experience.

BOOKINGS

A non-refundable 50% deposit is required to secure every booking. This deposit goes towards your full payment, and the remaining balance is due on the day of your appointment before your session begins.

  • Early & After-Hours Fee: Bookings earlier than 07:00 am and from 17:00 (5:00 pm) have an additional fee of R100.
  • Slot Availability: PLEASE CHECK IF SLOT IS STILL AVAILABLE on WhatsApp BEFORE PAYING DEPOSIT.
  • Outfit Policy: Outfit changes are NOT permitted after your makeup appointment.

CANCELLATION & RESCHEDULING

If you cancel your booking and have paid the deposit, please understand the amount is strictly non-refundable and cannot be applied towards future appointments.

  • 48-Hour Notice: Rescheduling is strictly permitted only 2 days (48 hours) prior to the scheduled appointment time.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours forfeit the full deposit fee.

TIME KEEPING & STUDIO ETIQUETTE

We operate on fixed time slots to ensure each client receives their full, uncompromised attention and luxury finish.

  • 20-Minute Grace: Please plan your travel accordingly. If you arrive more than 20 minutes late for your appointment, you will be charged a R100 late fee (or R100/30min deduction).
  • Automatic Cancellation: Your booking will be automatically cancelled if you are more than 30 minutes late.
  • Phone Etiquette: Once your session has started, please AVOID using your phone frequently as it distracts and delays the artist from finishing your look on time.
  • Guest Policy: Please AVOID bringing a friend or visitor with you to your appointment due to limited studio space and to prevent distractions during your glam session.
Fixed Time Slot Guarantee

Kindly note that we operate on fixed time slots so each client gets their full dedicated time. To avoid disruption to subsequent appointments, we kindly request punctuality.
