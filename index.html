Frequently Asked Questions

How do I secure my booking?

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking. Pay into First National Bank (PN Xaba, Account 63079093133, Branch 250655) and share proof of payment on WhatsApp.

What are your house call fees?

House calls are R300 for QwaQwa and Kestell, R350 for Harrismith, and R400 for Bethlehem. For Gauteng, house call fees range according to your exact location.

What is your late fee and cancellation policy?

We operate on fixed time slots. If you arrive more than 20 minutes late, a R100 late charge (or R100 per 30 minutes) applies. Arrivals over 30 minutes late are automatically cancelled and the deposit is forfeited. Rescheduling is strictly permitted at least 48 hours (2 days) prior.

Are there extra charges for early morning or late evening slots?

Yes. Appointments booked earlier than 07:00 am and from 17:00 (5:00 pm) have an additional fee of R100. Please always check slot availability before paying your deposit.

Are lashes and eyebrow shaping included?

Strip Lashes are available as an add-on for R50, and Eyebrow Shaping is available for R30.

Do you offer male grooming?

Yes! We offer Male Grooming and Male Makeup for R200, creating a clean, even-toned, camera-proof finish for grooms, photoshoots, and graduations.
